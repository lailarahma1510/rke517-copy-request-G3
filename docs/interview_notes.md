# Interview record: Ibu Ratna, Week 6 class (official, fictional)

This is the official record of what Ibu Ratna (head of the medical records unit, Klinik Harapan)
said in the Week 6 interview. Every rule in the PRD must come from this record or from
`client_message_CR.md`, or carry the label "(our assumption)". Do not change this file.

## Opening statement
"Good morning. I am Ratna, head of the medical records unit at Klinik Harapan. Almost every day,
patients and their families come to our counter and ask for a copy of their medical record: for
insurance, for a referral, or just to keep. Right now we write every request in a big book. Last
month the book went missing for two days, and one patient came back three times because her copy
was never ready. I want an app for these requests. It must be fast, and it must be safe. Can you help us?"

## Her answers

| Topic | Ibu Ratna's answer |
|---|---|
| Today's process, what goes wrong | "We write requests in a book. Nobody can see which requests are waiting. The book went missing for two days. Once, a cousin took a copy without any letter from the patient." |
| Who may request | "The patient, with KTP. Or a family member with a signed letter of authority (surat kuasa) and both KTPs. For a child under 17, a parent with the family card (KK)." |
| Insurance, lawyers, police | "Not in this app. They send an official letter and follow a different route." |
| What they can get | "A copy of the visit summary (resume medis), lab results or radiology reports. Never the original file." |
| Who uses the app | "Our counter staff record the request and check ID. I approve or refuse every request before copies are made. Filing staff prepare the copies." |
| Approval | "I approve every request myself. If I refuse, the reason must be written down." |
| "Fast" | "The copy is ready within 3 working days after I approve it. And when a patient phones, staff can tell them the status straight away." |
| "Safe" | "Check ID every time. No letter of authority, no copy. Write down who collected which documents, when, and which staff member handed them over. Only our unit can see the requests." |
| Collection, sending | "They come to the counter and sign when they collect. We do not send copies by WhatsApp or email." |
| Status of a request | "Received, Approved, Ready, Collected. Or Refused." |
| Payment | "Copies are free. Nothing about money in this app." |
| Reports | "Each month I would like to see how many requests we had and how many were late." |
| SMS, notifications | "Not now." |
| The old book | "Keep the book. Do not type old requests into the app." |
| Information to keep | "Request number, the patient's medical record number, who asked and their relationship to the patient, their NIK, which documents, the date, my decision and reason, and who collected it and when." |
| Anything else | "I'm not sure yet. Write it down as an open question." |
